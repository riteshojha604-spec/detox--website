Small things that can help

🌬️

One-minute breathing

Slowly breathe in for 4 seconds, then out for 6. Repeat several times without forcing it.

📔

Journal prompt

“What is one thing I can control today, and one thing I can let go of for now?”

🚶

Change your setting

Stretch, walk around, drink some water, or step somewhere quieter for a few minutes.
